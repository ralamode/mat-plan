  skipping or forgetting them** (`PRIV-3`; [runbooks.md](../runbooks.md) → "Erase one athlete"). The
  report gains two parts:

    program. The household chooses: keep, remove and keep the copy, or remove both.

    gone, such as a label written about one child. Each is kept or removed by a correction.

  answer, and asks again about a copy made during the cooling-off. Both items were raised by lane P's
  privacy review (6a-iii) and are owed before copy-a-workout ships (7a-ii). No roadmap item moved.
