Movement picker: filters, near matches and “How to”

Prototype for the movement library, version 2. It works: type, filter, tick, press Enter. The data is the batches drafted so far (277 movements) and three made-up custom items.

Four choices, decided: the first option of each

Left here so you can still flip one and compare.

Decided by you (17 Nov)

  1. Your own movements are called “Your custom items”.
  2. Both sections, your custom items and all movements, fold like a tree.
  3. A filter choice that would find nothing is shown greyed and can’t be ticked.
  4. A four-letter word may be one letter off when its first two letters match (“pusg” finds Push-up). Shorter words must be exact.
  5. Ticking equipment lists movements that use at least one of them; every row shows what it needs.
  6. Your custom items stay reachable while you filter, without pushing the filtered results down: folded to one line when you browse, and listed after the filtered matches when you search. A name you typed exactly still comes first.
  7. All movements are grouped by kind of training, then family.
  8. Results are ordered best match first: an exact name; then names that start with what you typed; then a word that starts with it; a match in the middle of a word last. A movement’s own name before its other name, and a shorter name before a longer one.
  9. Typing three letters or more of another name finds the movement (“military” finds Overhead press), ranked by the same rule.
  10. A “How to” button on each row opens the instructions in the picker.
  11. An abbreviation that spells a movement is that movement: “db row” says “Already in the list as Dumbbell row” and offers no new item.

The rest: one yes covers these

  1. Filters are one button and a panel. Equipment is one choice of three, so nothing you ticked is ever cleared for you. Equipment is listed by how many movements use it, ten first.
  2. “No equipment” means just you and the floor: a wall, a towel or a partner counts as equipment.
  3. Muscles use plain words and filter by the main muscle: “Inner thigh”, “Outer hip”, “Upper traps”, “Forearms and grip”.
  4. Each row shows how it is logged and what it needs (“Reps · Pull-up bar”), not which group it is in.
  5. An exact match is always listed first, even when it is not in your filters, and says so.
  6. When filters hide every match, the screen says how many and offers to clear the filters. It never shows movements your filters exclude.
  7. A typo gets “Did you mean”, apart from “Has some of your words”. Enter ticks an exact match, or the only match when it matched by its own name; never a “Did you mean” row or an “also called” row.
  8. The supervision label reads “Learn with a coach present”: under the name on the row, and again at the top of the instructions.
  9. Wording: “not in your filters”, “Did you mean”, “Tick items to add”.
  10. Filters stay on while you keep adding to the same workout and reset when you leave it. “Recent” is hidden while you filter. A group opens itself when 20 or fewer movements are left.
  11. When logging (choose one), a tap chooses the movement and closes the picker.

Not for sign-off here: the entries themselves (they are in the sheet), and the exact shade of each colour.
